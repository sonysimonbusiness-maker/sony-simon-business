Privacy notice.

Last updated October 30, 2026

This site is run by Sony, a one-person software and automation business. This page explains in plain words what the site collects and what happens to it. It is a plain-language notice, not legal advice.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email and message. I use it to reply to you.
  • Free downloads and the quiz: your email, which resource you asked for, the page you were on, the quiz answers if you took it, and the exact consent wording you agreed to. The quiz result shows without an email. A download or result never requires you to join the email list.
  • Calculators and tools: numbers you type stay in your browser. The checklist and trip log are saved only on your own device and are never sent to me.
  • Visit statistics: Vercel Web Analytics counts page views and general device and country information in aggregate. I do not use advertising cookies or sell data.

Who handles it

The site is hosted on Vercel. Form messages and signups pass through Make.com, which stores signups, and are emailed through Gmail. These providers process data only to run the site for me.

The website assistant (ask.html), when switched on, sends your question and the last few messages to Make.com and an AI service to write a reply. I do not store the chat on this site, but Make.com keeps a short run history. Do not type passwords or private details into it.

Email

I email you only if you wrote to me or ticked the box agreeing to emails. Every email will explain how to unsubscribe, and you can also write to me at any time. I do not send cold outreach from this site.

How long I keep it

I keep messages and signups until you ask me to delete them, or until they are no longer useful to me.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what I hold about you, or to unsubscribe, email sonysimon.business@gmail.com. I will act on it promptly.

Changes

If this changes, I will update the date above.
