Questions to ask every vendor

  1. What exactly is included, and what costs extra (changes, extra users, support, training)?
  2. Who owns the finished work, the data and the accounts if we stop?
  3. What happens to the price after the first year?
  4. How do I get my data out, and in what format?
  5. What is the cancellation rule, and is there a fee?
  6. Who do I contact when something breaks, and how fast is the reply?

How the math works

Total = one-time setup + monthly fee x months + per-user fee x users x months + yearly fee x years + other fees. It is a simple estimate from the numbers you type. It does not include tax, price rises, or costs nobody quoted.
