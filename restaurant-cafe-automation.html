Restaurants and cafes

Simple automation for restaurants and cafes.

Fewer missed calls, easier catering follow-ups and one view of the week, without a new complicated system.

The problem

The phone rings during the rush, catering inquiries wait until the end of the day, and reviews and reservations are handled between everything else.

Jobs I can automate

Missed calls

A text goes back asking if they want to order, reserve or ask about catering.

Catering inquiries

A simple form that collects the date, headcount and budget, with a quick reply.

Review requests

A polite request after a visit or an order.

Weekly summary

Inquiries, reviews and busy times in one short report.
