Quick answers

Things people usually ask.

How much does it cost?

I quote a fixed price after we talk, before any work starts.

How long does it take?

You usually see a working demo in one to two weeks.

Do I own what you build?

You own your data and the finished work, including the code I write for you. Hosting, domain and other accounts are opened in your name, so you're never locked in. Third-party tools stay under their own licenses. The details go in a short written agreement before work starts.

What happens after launch?

Software needs bug fixes and updates over time. Support after launch is optional and agreed in writing before work starts, so you know what's covered and what it costs.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts. I never ask you to send passwords in chat or email.

What does the AI actually do?

It handles routine work, like sending you a first reply. I read every message myself, and I review everything I build before you see it.
