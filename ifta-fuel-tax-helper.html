Tax rates change every quarter. Look up each state's rate in the official IFTA rate table at iftach.org, and enter it in dollars per gallon. The two example rows use made-up rates so you can see how it works.

Gallons used in a state = miles in that state divided by fleet miles per gallon. Tax due = (gallons used minus gallons bought) times the rate. A negative number is a credit. This is an estimate to help you check your filing. File through your base jurisdiction and keep your fuel receipts and mileage records.
